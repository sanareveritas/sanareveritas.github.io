What is Sanare?

We investigate health claims by tracing them back to their original evidence, evaluating the strength of that evidence, and publishing verdicts based on what the science supports.

Key Principles:

  • Investigate Claims Examine health claims circulating online and offline.
  • Evaluate Evidence Review scientific studies, systematic reviews, and trusted health sources.
  • Publish Verdicts Communicate what the evidence supports clearly and accessibly.

How SANARE Works

Step 1 — Claim

A health claim is identified from social media, news, advertisements, or community submissions.

Step 2 — Investigation

Scientific literature, guidelines, and primary evidence are reviewed.

Step 3 — Evidence Grade

The quality and strength of available evidence are assessed.

Step 4 — Verdict

A conclusion is published based on what the evidence supports.
