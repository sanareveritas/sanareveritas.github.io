About Sanare Veritas

Helping people distinguish between health claims and scientific evidence through evidence-based health investigations.

Vision

To become a leading youth movement in advancing health literacy across Indonesia and creating a society that relies on credible, evidence-based information.


Mission

  • Investigations & Education:
    Investigate popular health claims and communicate accurate medical information in clear and accessible language.
  • Community Outreach:
    Organize health education programs for schools, orphanages, and communities in need.
  • Social Impact & Donations:
    Conduct donation drives and humanitarian initiatives to improve the health and well-being of vulnerable communities.
  • Young Researchers Mentorship:
    Mentor students who are interested in research and the health sciences, helping them develop their knowledge and potential.

Programs

  • SANARE CARE:
    A community outreach program that promotes health through donations of food supplies, medical equipment, and health education for orphanages and underserved communities.
  • SANARE Goes to School (Outreach):
    An interactive school outreach program that educates students on how to identify health misinformation, evaluate reliable sources, and adopt healthier lifestyles.
  • SANARE Future Researchers (Mentoring):
    An exclusive mentoring program for students interested in scientific research, academic writing, medicine, and the health sciences.
